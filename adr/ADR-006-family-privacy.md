# ADR-006 — Family privacy

**Status:** Accepted · 2026-08-03

## Context
The real users are the author's children; drawings and likenesses may appear in a public
demo.

## Decision
Images live in a private bucket behind signed URLs; profiles carry first names only; any
drawing or on-camera appearance requires the student's explicit consent. The public demo
dataset consists of drawings shared with permission plus drawings made ad hoc by the author
with deliberate errors.

## Consequences
- The public repo and video contain nothing a family member did not agree to share.
